## Our Pledge

To foster an open and friendly environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Fluxweir project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that promote an open, inclusive, diverse, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include but are not limited to:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include but are not limited to:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at [Kirky-X@outlook.com](mailto:Kirky-X@outlook.com) (contact: Kirky.X). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these community impact guidelines in determining the consequences for actions they deem in violation of this code of conduct:

1. **Correction**: for violations with minor community impact and a clear willingness to comply, a private warning clarifies the nature of the violation and explains why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning**: for a series of violations the community maintainers deem continuing, a warning is issued, possibly accompanied by behavioral consequences over a period of time. Interaction with the people involved is not allowed for the designated period, solicited or not — including interactions in community spaces and external channels. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary Ban**: for serious community-impact behavior, including sustained misconduct, a ban is imposed for a designated period. During the ban, no public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed, solicited or not. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent Ban**: for patterns of sustained community impact, including harassment of individuals, insults or disparagement, or hate behavior toward certain groups, a permanent ban is imposed.
